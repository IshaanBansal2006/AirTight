# Decision 009: Fleet Sweep And Costs

## Context
- B6 sweeps "around twelve" configurations and B7 needs a cost per hour per agent type that C can cite; neither existed.
- The full grid (1 to 4 drones × Go2 × guard × charging policy) is 32 cells, too many for 200 seeds each by hour 20.

## Decision
Twelve configurations, generated by `scripts/make_fleet_configs.py`: drones 1 to 4 with Go2 and guard, each synchronized and staggered (8); two drones with Go2 removed, with the guard removed, and with both removed, all staggered (3); and a no-drone Go2-plus-guard reference (1). Baseline is two drones, Go2 and guard, synchronized. Staggered means drone offsets evenly spaced over the 65-minute drone charge cycle. Costs: drone 7, Go2 9, guard 32 USD per hour, derived in `scenarios/logistics_yard/costs.md`.

## Reason
- The drone-count axis with both charging policies is the cost-versus-detection curve and the before-and-after fix in one set; the three removal cells show what each agent type buys; the no-drone cell is the "before you buy a robot" point.
- Even spacing is the natural first stagger; B8's fix loop searches offsets from there.
- The cost figures are three-year amortizations plus operations; the ratios drive the chart more than the absolutes, and the table states what would move each row.

## Consequences
- 12 cells × 200 seeds × 4 families is 9,600 episodes per adversary set at hour 18; F5 halves it if the sim is slow.
- Every cost number is marked "verify" until a source is attached; nothing from the table goes on a slide before that.
- Adding a configuration is a one-line edit in the generator, then rerun and commit.
