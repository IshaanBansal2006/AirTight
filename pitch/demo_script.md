# Demo script (three minutes)

1. **Open on the map.** "This is a commercial yard: three ways in, one asset, two docks, one fixed camera. Two drones, a ground robot and a guard patrol it, and every one of them bids for work in the same auction."
2. **The red team.** Run `airtight-redteam search` output on screen. "Our adversary searched thousands of intrusions. Its best one waits for the moment both drones are docked and walks in the north gate." Point at the charging-window path on the vulnerability map.
3. **Replay A, the miss.** Lane A's clip: the intruder walks in during the charging window; the Go2 is dispatched too late.
4. **The score.** Cost versus detection. "Each point is a fleet you could buy. The baseline sits here. Adding a drone moves you here for this much per hour."
5. **The fix.** "The system proposes staggering the charge schedule. Same seeds, same adversary, re-attacked." Before and after chart. Read the re-attack number aloud.
6. **Replay B, the catch.** Lane A's clip of the same seed after the fix.
7. **Human attention.** "This is decisions per hour: how often a person had to say yes or no. That number is a cost, and we report it next to detection."
8. **(If C5 shipped) Cut the base link live.** The fleet keeps merging memory over the degraded link and the alarm still arrives.
9. **Close.** "We sell the score and the map before you buy a robot, and we re-score after."
