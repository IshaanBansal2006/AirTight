    Glossary

    Caught in time
    The alarm reached a person early enough for a response to arrive before the intruder reached the asset.
    Operating point
    The alarm threshold at which the fleet raises one false alarm per hour on a quiet night; every score here is read at that threshold.
    Worst attack found
    The single attack the red team found that this configuration catches least often; the score for it is the floor, not the average.
    Critical detection point
    The last moment an alarm still leaves the response time before the intruder reaches the asset; an alarm after it is a miss.
